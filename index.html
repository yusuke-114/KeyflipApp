Keyflip

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · macOS app · bundle ID oneclick.keyflip.jp

Summary

Keyflip is a local macOS utility. It switches your keyboard input source automatically when you move to another app, using rules you set up. The app does not create accounts, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and is sandboxed without network entitlements. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information, and Keyflip never reads what you type — because the app only changes which input source is active, it has no reason to.

Information the app uses on your Mac

  • Input sources. Keyflip reads the list of input sources you have enabled in System Settings and which one is currently selected, and selects another one through Apple’s public Text Input Sources API. It does not read keystrokes or text.
  • The active app. macOS tells Keyflip which app has just become active (its bundle ID and name) so the matching rule can be applied. This is not recorded anywhere except in the rules you create. Switching events may be written to the macOS system log on your Mac for troubleshooting; that log stays on your Mac.
  • Window position. To show the input source indicator inside the app you switched to, Keyflip reads the size and position of that app’s front window. It does not read window titles or contents, and it does not need Screen Recording permission.
  • Apps you add. When you choose an app in the system file picker, Keyflip reads its bundle ID and name, with read-only access to that app only.
  • Preferences. Your rules (app bundle ID, app name, and input source ID) and settings are stored locally in standard UserDefaults inside the app’s sandbox container. Deleting the app and its container removes them.
  • Launch at login. If you turn this on, Keyflip registers itself as a login item with macOS. You can turn it off in the app or in System Settings.

The app does not access Accessibility, Input Monitoring, Screen Recording, iCloud, Contacts, Photos library, Location, Camera, Microphone, or the network.

What we do not collect

  • No names, emails, IDs, or account data
  • No keystrokes or typed text
  • No analytics, crash reporters, or advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No information about the apps you use sent off-device

Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties. Distribution through the Mac App Store is handled by Apple under Apple’s own policies.

Children

Keyflip does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
